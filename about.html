Vision

A world where industry and environment coexist.

MUSA Green has a vision to minimize the impact of climate change and make sure the availability of air, water, and energy for current and future generations.

We put our focus on solving the environmental problems in the industrial sector, the largest source of pollution in Indonesia, by helping industries manage their environmental resources and reduce pollution through the optimization of IoT and Big Data.

With core services on IoT (Monitoring & Control) and Data (Advanced Analytics & AI), and multiple MUSA product breakdowns. Integrator, Data Management, View, and Ad-hoc AI, we initiate and accelerate the sustainability transformation in the industries.

Mission

What we do, every day.

Monitor continuously

Deploy IoT sensors and edge devices that capture environmental data 24/7, air emissions, water quality, ambient air, energy, at the granularity the regulator demands.

Analyze intelligently

Turn raw telemetry into actionable insights with advanced analytics, dispersion models, water balances, and AI-powered anomaly detection.

Report automatically

Generate regulatory submissions (SISPEK, SPARING, ISPU) and sustainability disclosures (GRI, CDP, PROPER) directly from live data.

Visualize in 3D

Build Environmental Digital Twins that let operators, executives, and auditors see the same reality, in real time, in context.

Reduce pollution

Help industries identify and act on pollution sources, not after the fact, but as they happen. Prevention over remediation.

Accelerate transformation

Make sustainability transformation achievable, not a multi-year IT project, but a deployment that goes live in weeks.

The MUSA Stack

Four products. One pipeline.

Our product architecture follows the data from the sensor to the decision. Each product is a layer in the pipeline, and each one works independently or as part of the full stack.

MUSA Integrator

The edge layer. On-site data logger that connects to every sensor. CEMS analysers, water quality probes, AQMS stations, energy meters. Collects, signs, and pushes telemetry to the warehouse. Works offline; syncs when the link is back.

  • Modbus, SDI-12, 4-20mA, MQTT, OPC-UA
  • Edge buffering with store-and-forward
  • Tamper-evident data signing

MUSA Data Management

The warehouse layer. Cloud or on-prem data platform that stores, organises, and serves all environmental telemetry. Handles retention policies, backup, API access, and data governance. The single source of truth for every solution.

  • Time-series optimised storage
  • Hot/warm/cold tiering (18 months to 10+ years)
  • REST and GraphQL APIs

MUSA View

The platform layer. Environmental Digital Twin, live dashboards, compliance views, dispersion models, water balances, carbon ledgers, and embeddable reports. The operator's cockpit and the board's dashboard, from the same data.

  • Real-time monitoring with limit-aware colour coding
  • AERMOD-style dispersion & water balance models
  • Multi-tenant for industrial estates

MUSA Assistant

The intelligence layer. An AI agent that sits on top of the warehouse data. Ask questions in natural language, via Telegram, email, or the platform, and get sourced, contextual answers. "Why did stack A1 spike at 03:00?" → instant analysis.

  • Natural language queries over live data
  • Telegram, email, and in-platform chat
  • Sourced answers with data references
