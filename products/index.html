Products

The MUSA stack, from edge to agent.

Four products that cover the full environmental data pipeline. Deploy them together or plug them into what you already have. Every product connects to the same warehouse, speaks the same API, and shares the same compliance logic.

Edge · Layer 02

MUSA Integrator

On-site data logger that collects sensor data at the edge. Works offline, cryptographically signs every reading, and pushes to the warehouse on reconnect. The foundation of the pipeline.

Learn more
Warehouse · Layer 03

MUSA Data Management

Cloud or on-premises data warehouse that stores, organises, and serves all telemetry. Hot, warm, and cold storage tiers. REST and GraphQL APIs. The single source of truth.

Learn more
Platform · Layer 04

MUSA View

Environmental Digital Twin, live dashboards, compliance views, dispersion models, and automated reports. The operator's cockpit for air, water, energy, and carbon.

Learn more
Agent · Layer 05

MUSA Assistant

Personal AI agent that answers natural language questions over live environmental data. Available via Telegram, email, and in-platform. "Why did COD spike?", answered in seconds.

Learn more

Why MUSA

Products built for the real world.

Works with what you have

MUSA Integrator connects to any sensor with a standard output. No rip-and-replace. Plug into your existing CEMS, AQMS, or water probes and start streaming.

Cloud or on-prem

MUSA Data Management runs on GCP, Tencent Cloud, or your own rack. Choose the deployment model that matches your data sovereignty requirements.

Digital Twin ready

MUSA View turns compliance data into a live 3D environment. Dispersion plumes, water balances, carbon ledgers, visualised on a real map of your site.

AI-native from day one

MUSA Assistant isn't bolted on, it's a first-class layer. Ask questions in Bahasa or English, get sourced answers with timestamps and sensor IDs.

Data integrity guaranteed

Every reading is cryptographically signed at the edge. Tamper-proof audit trail from sensor to regulator submission. KLHK-compliant by design.

Start small, scale fast

Deploy one product or the whole stack. Add layers as your compliance obligations grow. The architecture stays the same, only the scope changes.
