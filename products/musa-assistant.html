Overview

ChatGPT for your environmental data.

MUSA Assistant is an AI agent that lives on top of your environmental data. It connects to the MUSA Data Management warehouse and understands the full context of your sites, sensors, and compliance obligations. Your HSE manager can ask "Why did COD at outfall SW-02 spike at 09:42 on Tuesday?" and get a sourced answer with data references — not a dashboard to interpret, but a direct answer with evidence.

The Assistant knows your estate: which stacks belong to which permit, what the regulatory limits are for each parameter, when the last calibration was due, and who needs to be notified when something drifts. It speaks English and Indonesian. It works at 3 AM on a Sunday before a regulator deadline.

This is Layer 5 of the MUSA stack. MUSA Integrator collects the data, MUSA Data Management warehouses it, MUSA View visualises it — and MUSA Assistant makes it conversational.

Features

What it can do today.

Natural language queries

Ask about any parameter, any site, any time range — in plain language. Understands Indonesian and English. Context-aware: remembers your estate, your stacks, your permits.

  • Multi-turn conversations with memory
  • "Compare SO₂ at A1 vs A7 this month"
  • Follow-up without re-stating context
Interface: Telegram chat

Sourced answers with data references

Every answer links back to the raw sensor data. See the exact reading, the exact time, the calibration state at that moment. No black-box responses.

  • Inline citations to warehouse timestamps
  • Calibration-status flag on every reading
  • One-tap "show me the raw data"
Answer with data source citation

Anomaly detection & alerts

Proactive monitoring: "Stack A1 SO₂ is trending 15% above the 7-day average" — flagged before it becomes an exceedance. Configurable alert thresholds per parameter per site.

  • Push alerts when trends drift
  • Thresholds per permit, per parameter
  • Weekly digest option for management
Alert: trend anomaly detected

Report drafting

"Draft the Q2 emissions summary for stack A1 through A5." Assistant pulls data, applies emission factors, and produces a draft with tables and narrative. Human reviews; AI does the heavy lifting.

  • GRI, CDP, KLHK-aligned templates
  • Emission factor auto-application (IPCC, KLHK)
  • Export to DOCX or PDF for review
Draft: Q2 emissions summary

How it works

From zero to answering questions.

01

Connect & onboard

Warehouse connection, site registry, user accounts, and role setup. Assistant gets read access to the MUSA Data Management warehouse and learns your sensor inventory.

02

Train on your context

Permit limits, alert thresholds, preferred report formats, and stakeholder roles. The more context you give, the sharper the answers.

03

Go live on Telegram

HSE team starts asking questions. No training required — just type. The assistant understands natural language from day one.

04

Iterate & expand

Custom alert rules, report templates, and new data sources over time. Assistant learns how your team works and gets better every week.
