Overview

The single source of truth between sensors and platforms.

MUSA Data Management is the data warehouse that sits at the center of the MUSA pipeline. The Integrator pushes data here; View, Assistant, and analytics workloads pull from it. It is the single source of truth, every sensor reading, every calibration event, every submission receipt, stored, indexed, and available through APIs.

Deploy it as a managed cloud instance on GCP or Tencent Cloud, or run it on-premise for estates with air-gapped or low-latency requirements. The warehouse is regulation-aware out of the box: KLHK retention periods, audit logging, and data-lineage tracking are built in, not bolted on.

Every solution in the MUSA catalogue SISPEK stack emissions, SPARING wastewater, ISPU ambient air, carbon ledgers lands in Data Management first. From there, it is queried by View dashboards, Assistant agents, and third-party BI tools through the same REST and GraphQL endpoints.

How it works

From provisioning to production in four steps.

1. Provision & configure

Cloud project setup, tenant creation, security policies, network peering (if on-prem). Storage tiers sized to your sensor count and ingestion rate. Done in days, not months.

2. Connect edge sources

MUSA Integrator pairing, sensor registry population, data model mapping. Each stack, outfall, AQMS node and meter is registered with its metadata, location, and regulatory context.

3. Validate & reconcile

Data quality checks, gap detection, calibration metadata import. We reconcile the first 30 days of data against source records to confirm completeness and accuracy.

4. Operate & scale

Continuous monitoring, retention lifecycle management, quarterly compliance reconciliation. Add new sensors and sites without re-architecture. The warehouse grows with your estate.
