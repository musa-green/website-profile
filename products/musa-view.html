Overview

One platform. Every parameter. Every stakeholder view.

MUSA View sits at the top of the MUSA pipeline. The Integrator pushes data, Data Management warehouses it; View is where the data becomes a working environmental twin - for the operator on the shop floor, the HSE manager, the kawasan executive, and the auditor.

Every solution in the MUSA catalogue surfaces here: CEMS stack cards, SPARING outfall trends, ISPU ambient maps, carbon ledgers, GRI report drafts - all driven off the same warehouse, all reconciled to the same regulatory baseline. No swivel-chair between tools, no exported CSVs.

Where it matters, View is more than a dashboard. Dispersion models, water-balance models and emission factors are first-class objects: change an input and watch the twin recompute downstream KPIs in real time.

Features

What you get out of the box.

Live monitoring dashboards

Real-time views per stack, outfall, AQMS node, energy meter and sub-station. Limit-aware colour coding, exceedance timeline and a 24-hour replay on every parameter.

  • Configurable per role (operator / HSE / exec)
  • Shift-handover snapshot export
  • Mobile-responsive for field use
Screenshot: live monitoring grid

Compliance views

Pre-built screens for SISPEK, SPARING, ISPU, PROPER. Each one reconciles raw telemetry against the regulator's expected cadence and limits - green is green only if the regulator agrees.

  • Submission log with signed receipts
  • Gap detection & back-fill workflow
  • Auditor share-link with read-only scope
Screenshot: SISPEK compliance view

Modelling - dispersion & water balance

Run AERMOD-style dispersion against your stack inventory. Build a water balance across intake, process, treatment and discharge. Change one input; downstream impact recomputes live.

  • Scenario versioning & comparison
  • Emission factors library (IPCC, KLHK)
  • Export model outputs to BI tools
Screenshot: dispersion model viewport

Embeddable Environmental Performance Reports

EPR pages render directly from live data - embed them in tender packages, sustainability microsites or board portals. Always up to date, always sourced.

  • GRI / CDP / PROPER-aligned blocks
  • Versioned snapshots for reporting periods
  • Public or token-gated embeds
Screenshot: EPR embed preview

Multi-tenant - built for kawasan

Operate a single MUSA View tenant for the whole estate, with isolated views per tenant factory. Every plant manager sees their own stacks and outfalls. The kawasan team sees the rollup and the cross-tenant compliance heatmap.

  • Role-based access & data scopes
  • Per-tenant branding for portal pages
  • Cross-tenant benchmarks (opt-in)
Screenshot: kawasan rollup - compliance heatmap
