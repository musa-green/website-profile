Overview

The bridge between sensors and the cloud.

The MUSA Integrator is an on-site data logger purpose-built for environmental telemetry. It sits at the boundary between the physical world (sensors, analysers, meters) and the digital world (data warehouse, platform, AI). Every reading from every sensor passes through it — it collects, timestamps, signs, and forwards. If the network drops, it buffers locally and resumes when the link is back. No data lost, no gaps.

Designed for the harsh realities of Indonesian industrial sites: dust, heat, intermittent power and unreliable cellular coverage. The Integrator ships with enough local storage to buffer 30 days of 1-minute data — more than enough to survive a monsoon outage.

It connects to any sensor out of the box: CEMS gas analysers over Modbus, water quality sondes over SDI-12, energy meters over pulse counters, AQMS stations over MQTT. One device, every protocol, every site.

Features

Built for the site, not the showroom.

Universal sensor connectivity

Modbus RTU/TCP, SDI-12, 4-20mA analog, pulse counters, MQTT publishers, OPC-UA. Connects to any CEMS analyser, water quality sonde, AQMS station, energy meter, or flow meter out of the box.

  • 8× analogue inputs (4-20mA)
  • 4× RS-485/Modbus digital channels
  • 2× SDI-12 for water quality sondes
  • 4× pulse counter for flow/energy meters

Offline resilience

Stores up to 30 days of 1-minute data on local flash. When the network returns, auto-resumes with gap detection. Network loss never means data loss.

  • 32 GB eMMC local storage
  • Automatic gap detection & back-fill
  • Store-and-forward with priority queuing
  • Watchdog timer — self-recovers from hangs

Tamper-evident data signing

Every data frame is cryptographically signed at the edge. Regulators and auditors can verify data integrity from sensor to submission. No retroactive edits, no silent corrections.

  • Per-frame HMAC signature
  • Chain-of-custody log
  • Regulator-verifiable integrity proof
  • Tamper detection alerts via SMS/Telegram

SISPEK/SPARING direct push

Integrator connects directly to KLHK submission endpoints via cellular or ethernet. No intermediate server, no manual transfer. The same data your operators see is what KLHK receives.

  • Direct KLHK endpoint integration
  • Cellular (4G LTE) or ethernet backhaul
  • Submission receipt logging
  • Auto-retry on failed submissions

Deployment

From box to live data in four steps.

1. Inventory & spec

Sensor list, fuel types, communication infrastructure survey. We map every instrument on your site to an Integrator input and confirm the submission endpoints.

2. Configure & provision

Edge config pushed remotely. Sensor mappings, submission endpoints, alert rules — all provisioned before the device ships to site.

3. Install & commission

Physical mounting, sensor wiring, end-to-end test with KLHK sandbox. Your Integrator goes live with verified data flow on day one.

4. Operate & maintain

Remote health monitoring, firmware OTA updates, annual calibration support. The Integrator runs itself — we keep it sharp.
