The problem

Annual reporting is a 3-month scramble — every year.

Sustainability reporting in Indonesia is still largely a manual, annual exercise. HSE teams spend 2–3 months each year collecting data from spreadsheets, lab reports and operator logs — then hand it to a consultant who produces a PDF. The result is expensive, slow, and disconnected from what's actually happening on site.

  • Data latency — By the time the report is published, the data it describes is 4–6 months old. The board makes decisions on stale information.
  • Consultant dependency — Every cycle means re-briefing an external team. Knowledge doesn't accumulate — it leaks.
  • One-audience bias — The same PDF is sent to the board, the regulator and the public, even though each needs a very different level of detail.

"The consultant's PDF is a photograph. Your telemetry is a live stream."

Overview

From annual snapshot to continuous disclosure.

MUSA's Sustainability Report solution generates GRI-aligned, PROPER-aware disclosures directly from the live data in your MUSA View warehouse. Environmental performance, energy consumption, water balance, community engagement — all pulled from the same telemetry that drives your daily compliance. The report updates continuously, so by the time the reporting period closes, the draft is already done.

We map your material topics to GRI Standards disclosure blocks, configure the KPI pipelines, and generate the first draft for your internal review. No consultant re-briefing. No spreadsheet archaeology. The board gets a live dashboard; the regulator gets a formatted PDF; the public gets a web view — all from the same source of truth.

PROPER scoring, CDP questionnaire pre-fill, and DJSI-facing metrics are all derived from the same dataset. When a rating agency or investor asks for data, the answer is already in the system.

Process

From materiality to first draft in 6–8 weeks.

01

Materiality assessment & scope

Identify your material topics through stakeholder interviews and regulatory mapping. Define report boundaries, KPI list and framework alignment (GRI, PROPER, CDP).

02

Data integration & KPI mapping

Connect live telemetry feeds to each KPI. Map manual data sources (HR, finance, community) to the disclosure framework. Build the automated pipeline.

03

First draft generation & review

The system generates the first complete draft. Your team reviews, adds narrative context, and approves. Stakeholder views are configured for board, regulator and public.

04

Publish & continuous update

Publish the report. KPIs continue updating in real time. Next period's draft is already forming — no scramble, no consultant re-brief.
