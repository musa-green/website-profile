Overview

The shortest path from stack to regulator.

SISPEK (Sistem Informasi Pemantauan Emisi Industri secara Kontinu) is the Indonesian government's continuous emission reporting framework. Every stack that falls under PermenLHK No. 13/2021 must stream sub-hourly readings to KLHK - gas concentrations, opacity, flow, temperature, oxygen - all tied to a registered identity.

MUSA's CEMS program handles the full chain. We size the analyser package against your fuel mix and combustion profile, install in-stack and extractive systems with a shelter, run RATA/CGA calibration, and connect the certified data feed to SISPEK. From day one your operators have the same view as the auditor - minus the lag.

The same telemetry feeds MUSA View, so emission performance sits next to throughput, energy and process variables. The annual environmental report writes itself.

Features

What's actually in the box.

Certified analyser package

In-stack and extractive configurations for SO₂, NOₓ, CO, CO₂, O₂, particulate matter, opacity, flow and temperature. Matched to your fuel and load profile.

  • EN 14181 / US EPA Method-equivalent
  • Shelter, sample conditioning, blowback
  • QAL2 / QAL3 calibration support
Photo: analyser rack inside shelter

Automated SISPEK submission

Edge logger pushes the certified data stream to KLHK on the regulator's cadence. Submission status visible to your team in real time - every send, every retry.

  • Direct integration with SISPEK endpoint
  • Retry, queue and replay on link loss
  • Signed submission receipts archived
Screenshot: SISPEK submission log view

Live stack dashboard in MUSA View

Every stack rendered as a card: emission concentration vs limit, trend, last calibration, last submission, and a 24-hour timeseries. Drill into any reading for the raw analyser frame and the conditioning state at that moment.

  • Limit-aware colour scale (Good / Warn / Exceed)
  • Shift-handover snapshot export
  • Comparable across stacks, plants or tenants
